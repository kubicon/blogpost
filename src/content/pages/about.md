I am Ondřej, a fourth-year PhD student at the Czech Technical University in Prague. My research revolves around solving large two-player zero-sum games with imperfect information. Since I am a big fan of deep learning and reinforcement learning, most of my research focuses on combining the two in games. Unrelated to game theory, I really like the concept of diffusion models, and I have played with both the image-generating and text-generating ones.

Outside of technology and research, I love watching movies and playing the electric guitar. I am also a beginner at origami folding, which I started very recently.
