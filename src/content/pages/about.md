I am Ondřej and I am a fourth year PhD student at Czech Technical University in Prague. My research revolves around solving large two-player zero-sum games with imperfect information. Since I am a big fan of deep learning and reinforcement learning, most of my research deals with the combination of the two in the games. Unrelated to the game theory, I really like the concept of diffusion models and I have played both with the image-generating one and also the text-generating one.

Outside of technology and research, I love watching movies and I play electric guitar. I am also a beginner at origami folding.
